Use case description

Aivota is a clinical platform serving children with special needs (currently focused on Rett's Syndrome) and their parents/guardians, clinicians, and special-education staff. We use Amazon SNS to deliver one-time passcodes (OTPs) for guardian-consent verification, as required under Israeli Privacy Protection Authority guidance (February 2026) and equivalent regulations (GDPR Article 8, COPPA, FERPA) governing the collection of medical/educational data on minors.

A guardian receives a magic link by SMS, clicks through to our consent page, and then enters a 6-digit code we send to the same phone number to satisfy the non-repudiation requirement. Without this two-channel verification we cannot legally onboard students.

All SMS traffic is transactional — there is no marketing, advertising, or opt-out-required content. We send only after a clinician (acting on behalf of the data subject) has explicitly initiated a consent request for that specific phone number; recipients have a prior off-band relationship with the clinic that requested the message.

Target countries

Primary: Israel (IL). Secondary: United States (US). Future expansion may include EU member states and the UK.

Estimated volume

- Year 1: ~500–2,000 SMS per month (one OTP request per consent transaction; each guardian onboards 1–3 times during account setup, plus occasional re-consent on policy version bumps).
- Burst capacity: <50 sends per hour (clinics typically batch onboard a small cohort at a time).
- Per-phone rate: capped at 5 OTP requests per 10-minute window in our service.

Sample message body

Your Aivota verification code is 123456. It expires in 10 minutes. Do not share this code.

(Sender ID: Aivota — separate registration submitted for IL.)

Opt-in confirmation

Yes — we only send SMS to phone numbers explicitly provided by a clinician on the recipient's behalf during a consent collection workflow that the recipient has been notified of through a prior channel (email, in-person, or phone call). The recipient has a pre-existing service relationship with the originating clinic. We do not source phone numbers from purchased lists, scraping, or any third party.

Opt-out handling

Because messages are exclusively transactional OTPs tied to a specific in-progress consent transaction, there is no recurring messaging to opt out of — each code corresponds to a single user-initiated verification attempt. Recipients can decline to complete the consent process at any time, which terminates all messaging to that number. Standard STOP/UNSUBSCRIBE keywords are honored at the carrier level.

Compliance posture

- HIPAA-eligible AWS services only (SNS is HIPAA-eligible; we'll have a BAA in place before processing PHI in production).
- All OTP codes are SHA-256 hashed at rest, expire in 10 minutes, and are rate-limited per phone number.
- No PHI is transmitted in SMS bodies — only the verification code.
- Consent records (including the SNS message ID returned by Publish) are audit-logged for evidentiary purposes per Israeli PPA Section 11.
