To integrate the Ministry of Education's identification system into your website, you must follow these official steps:

Vendor Registration: You must be an approved vendor providing educational services. You need to contact the ICT (Information and Communication Technology) Administration at the Ministry.

Technical Protocol: The system primarily uses SAML 2.0 or OpenID Connect (OIDC) for authentication. You will need to implement a Service Provider (SP) on your server to handle the identity assertions.

Data Security: You must comply with strict privacy regulations. This usually involves signing a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) and proving your site meets Israeli cybersecurity standards for student data.

Integration Environment: The Ministry provides a "Staging/Sandbox" environment to test the login flow before going live.

Useful Resources:

Vendors Portal: sapakim.education.gov.il (The technical documentation is mostly in Hebrew, so you may need a translation tool).

Support: Reach out to the Ministry's Sapakim (Vendors) Support Desk to initiate the technical "handshake."
