## CliniAACian — The Clinician & Caretaker Portal
 
### Your AI-Powered Clinical Partner
 
CliniAACian puts a context-aware AI assistant at the center of your workflow. It knows your students, understands their goals, and helps you make better decisions faster.
 
- **Conversational AI with full platform context** — ask questions, request insights, or manage data through natural conversation with voice or text.
- **Built-in medical knowledge base** — RAG-powered access to detailed information on medical conditions, clinical best practices, and FDA-approved medications, allowing the AI to offer expert service when needed.
- **Specialized AI personas** — Select from a variety of AI personas to customize your experience, depending on whether you are looking for a clinical assistant, a specialized expert, or a family doctor.
 
### Student Files That Build Themselves
 
Upload a PDF to transform it into structured, searchable clinical data in seconds — automatically extracted and organized across medical records, functional reports, and educational profiles.
 
- Medical records: diagnoses, medications, health alerts, equipment
- Functional reports: mobility, ADLs, sensory profiles, safety considerations
- Educational reports: communication modes, assistive technology, behavioral strategies
- Draft, review, and finalize with full version history
- Print-ready output for meetings, reports, and compliance
 
### Student Management at a Glance
 
Create and manage comprehensive student profiles with demographics, diagnoses, and background information. Search and filter instantly by name, ID, school, or status. Assign students across multiple institutes and track active or completed cases — all from one dashboard.
 
### A Calendar Built for Clinical Teams
 
Schedule sessions, meetings, recurring appointments, and classes — all fully integrated with the AAC experience. When a student's calendar says it's music class, their communication board adapts automatically.
 
### AAC Board Builder — Design Boards in Seconds
 
Describe what you need in plain language and let the AI generate a complete communication board. Then fine-tune it manually with a visual drag-and-drop editor or automatically by providing additional details.
 
- Multi-page boards with intuitive navigation
- Full button customization: labels, symbols, colors, and actions
- AI-generated custom symbols with a clinician approval workflow
- **Context-aware activation** — tag boards with situational hints (in class, mealtime, at home) and the AAC will automatically surface them based on the student's environment, camera input, and calendar
- Export to Grid 3 (.gridset) format for cross-platform compatibility
 
### IEP & TALA Program Management
 
Whether you work within the US IEP framework or Israel's TALA system, CliniAACian supports your full progress tracking workflow.
 
- Annual goals with baselines, measurable targets, and success criteria
- Short-term objectives with configurable measurement methods
- Automated data collection with trend visualization
- Related services tracking across speech, OT, PT, and more
- Team management with roles and contact information
- Meeting scheduling and consent form management
 
### Premium — Video Analysis
 
Upload session videos and receive an AI-generated behavioral timeline. The system identifies key events, tracks the student's focus and attention, and highlights moments that matter.
 
### Premium — Deep Analysis Mode
 
Go beyond surface-level data. Deep Analysis examines every piece of information across a student's profile — progress data, session notes, behavioral patterns, medical history — and surfaces insights that might otherwise be missed. The output is a detailed, actionable report with findings, progress assessments, and recommended next steps.

## The AAC — A Student Experience Like No Other
 
### Communication That Thinks Ahead
 
The AAC doesn't just display buttons — it anticipates what the student needs to say. AI-powered boards adapt in real time based on context, environment, conversation flow, and the student's own patterns.
 
- **Interactive Mode** — the AI engages directly with the student, asking questions and guiding progress toward clinical goals
- **Assist Mode** — the AI manages the board quietly in the background while the student communicates with others, stepping in only when needed
- **Silent Mode** — manual override to keep the AI fully silent
- Quick-access essentials: Yes / No / More / Home / Back
- Supports both pre-built boards and dynamically generated boards
 
### Every Way In — Multiple Input Methods
 
Not every student communicates the same way. The AAC supports the full spectrum:
 
- **Touch** — standard tap interaction
- **Eye gaze** — compatible with Tobii, EyeTech, LC Technologies, Gazepoint, WebHID, and browser-based trackers, with passive fixation-based calibration and configurable dwell sensitivity
- **Cursor** — mouse or switch-based navigation
- **Voice** — audio messages sent directly to the AI
- **Body gestures** — recognized through the camera in real time
 
### Sees What Words Can't Say — Gesture & Body Language Detection
 
The AAC's camera-based detection system reads the room so the student doesn't have to spell it out.
 
- Facial expressions: smiles, frowns, attention shifts
- Head gestures: nods, shakes, turns
- Hand gestures: waves, points, raises, pinches
- Sign language interpretation
- Object detection — identifies items the student is focused on and adds them to the board
- Multi-person detection — recognizes caregivers and clinicians in frame
 
### Passive Co-Listening
 
The AAC listens to ambient conversation and detects when caregivers offer choices or ask yes/no questions directed at the student — then automatically surfaces the right response buttons without anyone needing to set them up.
 
### Voice & Audio — Their Voice, Their Way
 
Choose from multiple TTS engines — ElevenLabs, Google Cloud, browser-native, and Gemini real-time voices. Configure separate voices for student output and AI responses. Adjust pitch, speed, and volume to match each student's preference.
 
### Built-In Apps — Learning Disguised as Play
 
Togglable and tunable from the clinician portal, each app is designed to engage students while generating meaningful clinical data.
 
- **Drawing Canvas** — supports eye-gaze drawing for students who can't hold a pen
- **Music Maker** — virtual piano with color-coded keys
- **YouTube Player** — large, accessible playback controls *(coming soon)*
- **Spotify Player** — AI-powered song suggestions *(coming soon)*
- **Farming Sim** — a cognitive training game designed with expert researchers to build skills and track progress *(coming soon)*
- **Curriculum Q&A** — a questions game integrated with school materials *(coming soon)*
 
### Accessibility at Every Level
 
- 5 levels of icon-to-text ratio adjustment
- Parts-of-speech color coding for language learning
- Dark and light themes
- 12 languages with full RTL support, including Hebrew and Arabic
- **Purpose-built for Rett Syndrome** — passive input, minimal-effort interaction, and fixation-based activation
 
### An Avatar That Cares
 
An animated companion responds to the student with emotional states — happy, sad, neutral, sleeping — and reacts to the student's attention level. A face mirror display shows the student their own detected expressions and hand positions, building self-awareness and engagement.
