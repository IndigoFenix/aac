# Aivota capability descriptions accessible to CRM chat

## Licenses

Aivota offers licenses for families, schools, and clinics. All licenses have the same basic features - the license type affects the display and organization only. (For example, families are expected to have fewer children than schools or clinics, and schools can be organized into classrooms.)

Licenses can be adjusted according to expected number of students, AAC use, and the inclusion or exclusion of other features depending on the user's needs.

## All licenses include:
- *CliniAACian, our Chat-Based Interface:* An AI-based natural language interface that allows the user to describe their needs. The AI can store or read information according to the user's request. All data management can be achieved through either traditional menus or by talking with the AI.
- *Student Context Awareness:* Our AI maintains a detailed memory of each student's capabilities, goals, activities and needs at all times, allowing it to offer specialized care.
- *Specialist AI Knowledge:* Our AI can pull from a massive library of specialist knowledge in the fields of medicine and special-needs education, including proprietary real-world evidence for specific conditions and direct access to drug information from the FDA.
- *Full HIPAA Compliance:* Privacy is Aivota's top priority. All data on our systems is stored with up-to-date, top-level security standards with special precautions taken to ensure that the AI cannot expose private information to unauthorized users.

## Optional CliniAACian Features:
- *Data Extraction from Reports:* Users may upload student reports, and the AI can pull information from them and store it in an organized file.
- *Video Analysis:* Users may upload videos of students, and the AI can analyze their behavior to find patterns and make conclusions.
- *Progress Planning:* The AI can guide users to creating a detailed goal-based plan for students based on their needs, using S.M.A.R.T. framework (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound). This plan can be updated based on new data and student progression and regression can be tracked, displayed, and analyzed over time.
- *Event Calendar:* Users can schedule events in the built-in calendar, which can be used to inform the AI of upcoming events and help enrich its context.
- *Deep Analysis Mode:* AI can search through student reports, schedules, and progress to produce a detailed analysis of their progress and needs, identify patterns and weak points that have gone unnoticed, and suggest future steps for parents, caretakers and therapists.
- *Automatic AAC Board Generator:* Users can use natural language to construct and edit AAC boards, and the AI can build them in minutes, generating images and navigational elements as needed.

## AI-Powered AAC:

We are building the next-generation AAC board system that uses a multimodal, context-aware AI to analyze surroundings and add new buttons to the board in real-time. Our aim is to create an AI-powered companion that can anticipate student intent and allow them to express themselves with a flexibility that would be impossible using a traditional board. It can also interact directly with the student, ask clarifying questions, suggest or provide activities based on student goals, monitor student progress, and report back to caretakers with new insights.
