The Legal Requirement (US/Israel)

US (FERPA/HIPAA): A school cannot legally see a private clinic's medical or behavioral reports without a specific "Release of Information" (ROI). A flat "Org-level" access would likely violate federal privacy laws the moment a student is shared between entities.

Israel (Privacy Protection Law): The "Minimum Necessary" principle applies. Even if a student is enrolled in both a school and an after-school program (Matnas), the school has no legal "need to know" regarding the specific behavioral program of the after-school entity.

The Ownership Solution
To avoid a massive architectural overhaul, we do not need a complex permission engine yet. We just need Object Ownership:

Student Record: Contains shared/common info.

Programs/Reports: Every object (Program or Report) must have an owner_institute_id.

The Rule: A user can only see or edit a program if the selected institute matches the program’s owner institute.

Why do this now?

Avoid Technical Debt: If we don't, we will eventually have to "split" student profiles to keep data private, creating a mess where the same student exists multiple times in our database.

Multi-Tenant Readiness: This allows a student to have a "Master Profile" while keeping the Clinic's data invisible to the School, and vice versa.

Summary: We should definitely account for the "Two Orgs, Two different Programs" scenario. It is not just a feature; in both the US and Israel, it is a compliance necessity.
